Qiāoyǔ (悄語) — Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: 28 August 2026
Developer / 开发者: Kaomi Dev Studio  ·  kaomiwings@gmail.com
App / 应用: Qiāoyǔ — Cultivate Clarity (package com.mindflow.app)

1. The short version / 摘要

Qiāoyǔ is a private journaling and mindfulness app. Everything you write, record, or photograph stays on your device. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and does not transmit your journal content, recordings, images, location, or birth details to us or to any third party.

悄語是一款私密的日记与正念应用。您撰写、录制或拍摄的所有内容都只保存在您的设备上。本应用没有用户账户、没有分析统计、没有广告，也不会将您的日记内容、录音、图片、位置或出生信息传输给我们或任何第三方。

2. Data the app stores (on your device only) / 应用在您设备上存储的数据

Data / 数据Purpose / 用途
Journal entries, moods, reflections
日记、心情、反思		Core journaling features. Stored in a local database on your device.
核心日记功能，存储在设备本地数据库中。
Voice recordings & transcriptions
语音录音与转写文本		Voice notes. Recorded only when you tap record; saved locally. Speech-to-text runs via your device's speech service.
语音笔记。仅在您点击录音时录制并保存在本地。语音转文字由设备自带的语音服务完成。
Photos & extracted text (OCR)
照片与识别文字		Photo thoughts. Text recognition runs fully on-device (Google ML Kit).
照片想法。文字识别完全在设备上进行（Google ML Kit）。
Approximate/precise location (optional)
位置（可选）		Only if you enable location tagging for a thought. Stored locally with that entry; never transmitted.
仅当您为某条想法开启位置标记时使用，随该条目保存在本地，绝不上传。
Name, birth date/time/place (optional)
姓名、出生日期/时间/地点（可选）		Used on-device to personalize daily wisdom. Never transmitted.
仅在设备上用于个性化每日智慧内容，绝不上传。
PIN & biometric app lock
PIN 与生物识别应用锁		Your PIN is stored locally. Biometric verification is performed by the Android system; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint/face data.
PIN 保存在本地。生物识别由 Android 系统完成，应用不会接触或存储您的指纹/面部数据。

3. Data we collect on our servers / 我们在服务器上收集的数据

None. Qiāoyǔ has no backend. The app's cloud backup is disabled at the system level (allowBackup=false), so your journal is not copied to Google's device backup either. If you want to move your data, use the in-app export feature — the exported file goes wherever you choose to share it.

无。悄語没有后端服务器。应用还在系统层面关闭了云备份（allowBackup=false），因此您的日记也不会被复制到 Google 设备备份中。如需迁移数据，请使用应用内导出功能——导出的文件由您自行选择保存位置。

4. Permissions / 权限说明

Microphone (voice notes), Camera (photo thoughts), Location (optional thought tagging), Notifications & alarms (your daily reminders), Biometrics (app lock). Every permission is requested only when you use the feature, and each is optional.

麦克风（语音笔记）、相机（照片想法）、位置（可选的想法位置标记）、通知与提醒（每日提醒）、生物识别（应用锁）。所有权限仅在您使用相应功能时请求，均为可选。

5. Children / 儿童

Qiāoyǔ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone (see section 3).

悄語不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会收集任何人的数据（见第 3 节）。

6. Deleting your data / 删除数据

Use Settings → Clear All Data in the app, or simply uninstall the app. Because nothing leaves your device, this removes everything.

在应用内使用"设置 → 清除所有数据"，或直接卸载应用。由于数据从未离开您的设备，此操作即可删除全部数据。

如本政策有变更，更新版本将发布于本页面并注明新的生效日期。如有疑问请联系：kaomiwings@gmail.com。
